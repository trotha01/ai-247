Lingua · Latin, Greek and Hebrew, one verse at a time

Each lesson takes one short verse, shows it with a transliteration and a word-by-word gloss (lemma and parsing), and reads it aloud: first slowly, then at normal speed, then in English. Latin, Greek and Hebrew take turns.

connecting

Everything here is generated by software from open texts and data: voices are synthetic and the parsing is automatic, so it can be wrong. Check a grammar before you rely on it.
