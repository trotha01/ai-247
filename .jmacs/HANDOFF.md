# Handoff

## Objective
AI-247: hourly cadence with >= 60 min of new footage per run (Trevor's rule: produced minutes >= cadence minutes), playlist prefers fresh bulletins and falls back to replays only when fresh footage is exhausted. Branch `cloud/ai247-coverage`; PRs #33 and #34, both squash-merged to `main` (9d5ec65, ab07c67).

## Done
- `hourly-bulletins` workflow (`.github/workflows/hourly.yml`, cron `23 * * * *`): plan job (one feed fetch over every working candidate feed, aired-URL memory from the per-bulletin manifests in R2, dedupe, never-aired stories first, 6 slices x 14 stories, <= 4 per source) -> render matrix (6 x 10-minute bulletins in parallel, unchanged `newsbot.day1` pipeline via `--items-json`) -> stitch (publishes all rendered bulletins in slice order as the fresh blocks of one root manifest, coverage check, job summary). `HOURLY_LLM_SLICES` (default 1) bulletins use Claude Haiku, the rest are extractive. `day1-episode` keeps `workflow_dispatch` only. Install steps in hourly.yml and ci.yml are bounded per stage with fast-failing pip and 20-minute budgets after the download stall hit 4 of 12 render jobs and 3 ci checks today.
- Worker + Python: manifest field `loop_from` (fresh blocks play once from `cycle_start_k`, then slate + the previous hour's bulletins loop); swap rule: finish the old fresh region if still ahead, else take over at k + margin inside the replay loop; a pending manifest's fresh blocks are inherited instead of dropped; legacy manifests unchanged. Same math in `worker/src/playlist.js` and `newsbot/channel.py`, cross-checked on generated vectors; hls-parser simulations of on-time and late runs. `publish_many` uploads 12 in parallel (613 objects / 195 MB in 80 s).
- Verified (docs/PLAN.md "Hourly cadence" and "Verification runs"): run 1 on main 20 min (Day 1 story floor of 10 rejected 3 full bulletins with 9 stories; 1 install stall), run 2 30 min (floor fixed; 3 install stalls), **run 3: 6 of 6 bulletins, 60.0 min of new footage published in 15 min**, run 2's pending 3 bulletins inherited in front (90-min fresh region). Swaps verified on air (16:51:26Z and 17:11:26Z: continuous media sequence, discontinuity sequence = the published anchor) and https://ai-247.pages.dev played through both (headless Chromium, state live, 12 s of video in 12 s, 0 errors). Cost: 0.0058 USD per run with one Haiku slice = ~0.14 USD/day, ~4.2 USD/month; the 0.05 per-process cap and the 10 USD monthly cap are unchanged.
- Coordination: built on the cron worker's PRs #25/#30 (Trigger logging, cron switch variable `HOURLY_CRON_ENABLED`); merged PR #29 (topics) into the branch, kept its topic test; the hourly slices run with `NEWSBOT_TOPIC_MODE=off` and `NEWSBOT_MAX_RUN_USD=0.05` (set by the topics worker on main).
