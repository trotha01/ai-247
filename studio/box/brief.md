Puddle Park is an original always-on preschool channel (ages 2-5). Warm, gentle and playful.

Look: a mix of live-action felt puppets on a bright handmade set (felt houses, a cardboard rainbow, a little
blue paper pond, a painted sunny sky) and short cartoons in varied handmade styles (flat hand-drawn, paper
cutout, crayon, watercolor, felt stop-motion).

Starting cast (keep them exactly as described; you may add new original characters):
- Pip: a small round orange fuzzy puppet with big white googly eyes, a tiny tuft of hair and a cheerful squeaky voice.
- Bramble: a tall shaggy green puppet with long floppy ears, a soft round nose and a slow, gentle deep voice.
- Wren: a purple felt bird puppet with a yellow beak, sparkly wing feathers and a bright sing-song voice.
- Biscuit: a yellow pom-pom puppy puppet with brown felt ears, a wagging tail and a playful giggly voice.

Teach through play: letters and their sounds, counting 1-10, shapes, colors, animal sounds, feelings, kindness,
simple routines (washing hands, tidying up), movement breaks and short sing-alongs. Mix segment types so it
feels like real TV: openers, lessons, cartoon shorts, songs, whimsical transitions and closers.
